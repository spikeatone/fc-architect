What is FC Architect?

FC Architect is a football-club management sim for iOS. You found a club at the bottom of one of the Big Five European league pyramids — England, Spain, Germany, Italy or France — then build a squad, play your matches, run the club's finances and climb season by season toward the top flight.

FAQ

How do I restore a subscription?

Open the app, go to the Pro upgrade screen and tap Restore Purchases. Restores run through the Apple Account that made the original purchase, so make sure that account is the one signed in on the device.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not by us: open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions — or visit apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions. Cancelling stops future renewals; you keep Pro until the end of the period you've already paid for.

Where are my saved games?

They are stored locally on your device, inside the app. Deleting the app deletes its saves, and because we never receive your game data we can't recover a save for you. If you back up your device, your saves are included in that backup.

What's free, and what needs Pro?

The full game is playable for free for your first complete season — nothing is locked away or trimmed down. Pro removes the season cap, so a career can carry on indefinitely.
