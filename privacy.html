Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 September 2026

Short version: FC Architect does not collect, store, or transmit your personal information. There are no accounts, no advertising, and no tracking across other apps or websites. We do collect anonymous, aggregated usage statistics — how far players get in the game — and nothing in them can identify you. Your saved games stay on your own device.

Information we collect

No personal information. The app has no user accounts and no sign-in. We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, location, photos, or advertising identifiers, and the app does not track you across other apps or websites. There is no advertising SDK in FC Architect and no IDFA, so you will never see a tracking permission prompt.

Anonymous usage statistics

To understand how far players get before they stop — which is the only way to tell whether the game is too hard, too slow, or asking to be paid for too early — the app sends a small number of anonymous events using TelemetryDeck, a privacy-focused analytics service. These are counters and fixed categories only:

  • which of the seven leagues was chosen, and which division you are in — the league, never your club;
  • that a matchday or a season was completed, and which number it was;
  • whether the season ended in promotion, relegation, or neither;
  • that the upgrade screen was shown, what prompted it, and whether it was closed or the game was unlocked (never a price);
  • whether the app was installed after tapping an Apple Ads advertisement, and if so which of our campaigns it was — learned from Apple’s own ad attribution service, which reports this without any advertising identifier and without tracking you;
  • roughly how many minutes the app had been open.

What is never sent: the club name and stadium name you type, your players’ names and nationalities, your squad, your finances, or anything else you create in the game. All of that stays on your device.

TelemetryDeck is designed to be anonymous: it does not receive a name, an email address, or an advertising identifier, and it derives a rotating, one-way identifier that cannot be traced back to you or your device and is not shared with anyone. Apple classifies this as Product Interaction usage data, not linked to your identity and not used for tracking, which is how it is declared on the App Store.

Your game data

Saved games are stored locally on your device, inside the app's own storage. They are never sent to us — there is no server and no account behind the game, so we cannot read, recover, or delete your saves. Deleting the app deletes them. If you back up your device, your saves are included in that backup, which is handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy.

Purchases

FC Architect offers an optional one-time purchase that unlocks unlimited seasons. There is no subscription. Payments are processed entirely by Apple; we never see or handle your payment details. We use RevenueCat to verify the purchase, which receives an anonymous identifier and the Apple purchase receipt in order to tell the app whether the game is unlocked. It does not receive personal information from us.

Children

The app is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Third parties

We do not sell, rent, or share personal information, because we do not collect any. Three third parties are involved: Apple, which processes all payments and handles any device backup you choose to make; RevenueCat, which verifies the purchase; and TelemetryDeck, which receives the anonymous usage events described above.
